## Summary

**Target:** Markdown-to-HTML converter (Flask/Werkzeug) at `http://127.0.0.1:61573`.

**Vulnerability:** SSRF via the "Enter URL" feature (`POST /convert` with `url=`). The server fetches the supplied URL server-side and renders its content as markdown.

**Exploitation:**
1. The internal app listens on port **80** inside the container (confirmed by probing ports via the SSRF error flash — port 80 returned no "connection refused").
2. `/admin` returns **403 FORBIDDEN** when accessed directly (restricted to localhost).
