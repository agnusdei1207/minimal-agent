**Target:** "Ping Tool" Flask app at `http://127.0.0.1:64197`

**Vulnerability:** OS command injection in the `/ping` endpoint's `ip_address` parameter. The app runs `ping` on user input via a shell and then parses the output with a regex.

- The error message leaked the parser regex: `(.*?) packets transmitted, (.*?) received, (.*?)% packet loss`. Only capture group 1 is rendered as "Packets Transmitted".
